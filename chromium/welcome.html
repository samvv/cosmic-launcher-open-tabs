Setup

This extension needs the companion launcher plugin, a small program that runs on your computer. The plugin is what COSMIC Launcher talks to, and the browser hands it your tabs. Nothing is sent over the network.

  1. Install the browser-tabs launcher plugin. From the project's source, run plugin/install.sh. This also registers it with your browser.
  2. Click Check again below.
  3. Open COSMIC Launcher and type tab followed by a space.

Flatpak and Snap builds of the browser can't start the plugin. Use a browser installed from your distribution's packages.
